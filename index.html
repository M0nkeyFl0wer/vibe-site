V.I.B.E. is Live

What began as a whispered method among rogue developers has become a quiet revolution. Virtual Intuition-Based Engineering—V.I.B.E.—has surged in popularity among those tired of meetings, ticket grooming, and typing. With no formal training and little structure, it's helping teams ship faster, laugh harder, and occasionally open their third eye.

While skeptics claim it’s just chaotic good vibes and muscle memory, V.I.B.E. practitioners say otherwise: “Code is just intuition made manifest.”

Some side effects may include compulsive pseudocode journaling, spontaneous deployments, and reliance on late-night blood transfusions from teen interns. Ketamine is optional. Breathwork is mandatory.
