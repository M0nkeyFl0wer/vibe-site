V.I.B.E.

Virtual Intuition-Based Engineering

What is Vibe Coding?

Vibe coding is a next-generation software development paradigm built on intuition, real-time iteration, and expressive momentum. Unlike traditional workflows, V.I.B.E. prioritizes experiential flow over rigid process — enabling teams to move faster, ship smarter, and resonate with purpose.

It started as a joke. Then a vibe. Then a pull request. Now it's a movement—one that values human rhythm over ticket systems, curiosity over KPIs, and the occasional nervous breakdown over Agile rituals.

Whether you’re pushing code from a cabin in the woods, channeling insight from your third eye, or just trying to fix the CSS for the 12th time—V.I.B.E. welcomes you.

Why V.I.B.E.?

  • Specs are optional. Energy is not.
  • Speed is a myth. Flow is measurable.
  • PRs are a feeling. Deployments are a vibe.

The Method

Instead of tickets, vibe coders use swipe concerns to determine project readiness. We replace estimation with resonance, and rely on decentralized intuitions to align toward collective shippability.

Ancient Origins

The first vibe coders were actually the Luddites. History tells you they broke machines — but what they really did was liberate the means of production with intention, intuition, and a desire to do less actual work.

Practices We Live By

  • Commit messages are written in poetry or scent descriptions.
  • Standups are replaced by shared breathing exercises across Figma canvases.
  • QA happens on the astral plane or live on Twitch.
  • You must get teen blood transfusions to code after midnight. Ketamine optional. Breathwork mandatory.
